Kanazawa 金沢
Mar 28–29 · Hyatt Centric Kanazawa · Two Full Weekend Days
28
Saturday · Arrive from Tokyo
Hyatt Centric Kanazawa
Full Day 🌸
Morning · Travel from Tokyo
  • Shinkansen to Kanazawa — Hokuriku Shinkansen from Tokyo Station (~2.5 hrs); buy an ekiben station bento for the ride
  • 🚉 Step off the train — your first Kanazawa stop is right at the exit
  • Tsuzumi-mon Gate at the station History — The iconic drum-shaped gate at the East Exit, modeled after tsuzumi hand drums used in Noh theater — a striking introduction to the city's arts heritage
Afternoon — One long walking loop
  • 🚶 10-min walk east from the station, following signs to the market
  • Omicho Market for lunch Food — Kanazawa's legendary covered market; order a kaisendon (fresh sashimi over rice) — the coastal waters here are famous for yellowtail, uni, and snow crab
  • 🚶 8-min walk east along Hyakumangoku-dori
  • Oyama Shrine Temple — Unique gate designed by a Dutch architect blending Japanese, Chinese, and European styles — the stained-glass top once served as a lighthouse for Sea of Japan ships. A beautiful koi garden inside
  • 🚶 5-min walk through the shrine grounds, cross the Nezumita-mon bridge directly into the castle grounds
  • Kanazawa Castle Park History — The restored white castle walls and watchtowers glow in the afternoon light. On Saturday evenings, the Gyokusen-inmaru Garden inside the park has a light-and-sound show
  • 🚶 2-min walk — Kenroku-en is directly across the street from the Ishikawa-mon gate
  • Kenroku-en Garden Nature — One of Japan's three most beautiful gardens. Spring brings early cherry blossoms among the ponds and teahouses 🌸 — admission is free during peak bloom
Evening
  • 🚶 15-min walk northeast, or 5-min taxi — Higashi Chaya is the neighborhood on the hill across the Asano River
  • Higashi Chaya Geisha District History — The best-preserved geisha quarter outside Kyoto; wooden ochaya teahouses line the lantern-lit streets at dusk — stop for matcha and gold-leaf sweets at one of the tea shops
  • 🍦 Vendors are on the main street and near the entrance
  • Kanazawa gold-leaf ice cream Food — A Kanazawa specialty: soft-serve wrapped in a sheet of edible gold leaf; available from vendors along the main street
  • 🚶 20-min walk or taxi back to hotel

Sunday · Last Day in Kanazawa
Hyatt Centric Kanazawa
Full Day
Morning
  • Head southwest from hotel — 20-min walk, or take the Kanazawa Loop Bus (Kenrokuen-Korinbo-direction) to the Korinbo stop, then 10-min walk
  • 🚶 Follow the Sai River south — the area feels like old Edo Japan
  • Myoryuji — The Ninja Temple Temple — Labyrinthine temple with hidden rooms, trapdoors, and secret escape routes. Book a guided tour in advance (essential — no walk-ins allowed)
  • 🚶 5-min walk through the quiet temple district
  • Nomachi neighborhood — Quiet historic district of temples and shrines; wandering these lanes feels like stepping into 1700s Japan
  • 🚶 10-min walk northeast along the Sai River toward the castle area
  • Nagamachi Samurai District History — One of Japan's best-preserved samurai districts; earthen walls, narrow canals and cobblestone lanes unchanged since the Edo period
  • 🚶 3-min walk inside the district
  • Nomura-ke Samurai Residence — A beautifully preserved mansion inside Nagamachi with a garden and upstairs tearoom (¥550 entry)
Afternoon
  • 🚶 15-min walk east — the museum is a circular white building right beside Kenroku-en
  • 21st Century Museum of Contemporary Art — Bold circular museum with interactive permanent installations; the swimming pool illusion room is unmissable (free outer grounds; ¥1,000 for inner galleries)
  • 🚶 5-min walk north into the castle park
  • Kenroku-en Garden (afternoon light) Nature — Return for a second visit in the golden afternoon light; the ponds and cherry trees look completely different than the day before 🌸
Evening · Final Night in Kanazawa
  • 🚶 15-min walk northeast across the Asano River
  • Higashi Chaya at night History — The geisha district feels even more atmospheric on a quiet Sunday evening with lanterns glowing along the wooden ochaya teahouses
  • 🍽️ Several excellent restaurants in and around Higashi Chaya
  • Farewell Kanazawa dinner Food — Try kaiseki or Kaga-style cuisine on your last night; Kanazawa is widely considered Japan's finest food city outside Kyoto
  • 🚕 Taxi back to hotel (~10 min) — early start tomorrow for the Shinkansen to Yokohama

Apr 4
Saturday · Day Trip — Kamakura
Hyatt Regency Yokohama (day trip)
Full Day 🌸
Morning
  • Travel from Yokohama to Kamakura — JR Yokosuka line from Yokohama Station (~35 min, ¥360). Aim to arrive by 8:30am before the weekend crowds 🌸
  • 🚶 5-min walk northeast from Kamakura Station
  • Tsurugaoka Hachimangu Shrine Temple — Kamakura's grand central shrine at the top of a long cherry-tree-lined avenue; the approach path (Wakamiya-oji) is one of Japan's most spectacular sakura tunnels 🌸
  • 🚶 15-min walk west through the backstreets, or 5-min taxi
  • Kotoku-in — The Great Buddha Temple — The 13-metre bronze Daibutsu seated amid cherry trees; one of Japan's most iconic sights. You can enter inside the statue for ¥20 extra
  • 🚶 10-min walk into the hills behind the Great Buddha
  • Daibutsu Hiking Trail Nature — A beautiful forested ridge trail connecting hilltop temples with views over Kamakura Bay; even a 30-min stretch is worth it. Full trail is ~90 min
Afternoon
  • 🚶 Walk or taxi back toward Kamakura Station (~20 min)
  • Hasedera Temple Temple — Hillside temple with a giant gilded Kannon statue and a garden terrace overlooking the sea; the hydrangea and cherry plantings are beautiful in early April 🌸
  • 🚶 5-min walk east along Komachi-dori
  • Komachi-dori lunch & browsing Food — Kamakura's lively main shopping street; excellent spots for soba, shirasu (fresh whitebait) rice bowls, and matcha soft-serve. A great place to slow down after the morning temples
Evening — Return to Yokohama
  • 🚇 JR Yokosuka line from Kamakura → Yokohama (~35 min)
  • Farewell dinner in Noge Food — End the Yokohama chapter in the city's beloved jazz-bar and izakaya district; find a smoky counter seat and let a local sake see you out right
  • 🚕 Taxi back to hotel (~10 min) — early start tomorrow for the train to Tokyo
